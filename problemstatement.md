Problem Statement

Project Title

AI-Powered Discovery Engine for Myntra Wishlist-to-Purchase Behavior

Background

Myntra users frequently save fashion products to their wishlist while browsing. However, adding a product to a wishlist does not always result in a purchase.

A user may wishlist a product because they genuinely intend to buy it, want to compare it with other options, are waiting for a discount, are unsure about size or fit, want feedback from others, or simply want to save the product for later.

For Myntra, this creates an important product discovery question:

What happens between “Add to Wishlist” and “Purchase”, and what prevents users from completing the purchase?

Problem Statement

Myntra users may experience multiple uncertainties after identifying a product they like, including concerns related to price, size, fit, quality, styling, reviews, occasion suitability, returns, and social validation.

At the same time, user feedback related to these concerns is spread across multiple public sources such as app reviews, Reddit, YouTube comments, social media, fashion communities, product reviews, and Q&A platforms.

Manually analyzing this feedback at scale is difficult.

Therefore, the problem is to build an AI-powered discovery engine that can analyze large volumes of publicly available user conversations and identify:

Why users add products to their Myntra wishlist

What stops or delays them from purchasing

Which uncertainties remain before purchase

Which barriers occur most frequently

Which user segments experience different problems

Which opportunity areas could most strongly influence wishlist-to-purchase conversion

Core Problem

Myntra needs to better understand why users who show interest by adding fashion products to their wishlist do not always convert that intent into a purchase.

Key Discovery Questions

The discovery engine should help answer:

Why do users add products to their Myntra wishlist?

Which wishlist additions represent genuine purchase intent?

When is the wishlist used only as a bookmarking tool?

What prevents users from purchasing a wishlisted product?

What causes users to postpone their purchase?

How important are price and discounts in purchase decisions?

How much do size and fit uncertainty affect purchase confidence?

How do ratings, reviews, and product quality concerns influence users?

How do users compare multiple wishlisted products?

What styling or occasion-related information do users need?

What information do users search for outside Myntra before purchasing?

How important is social validation from friends, influencers, Instagram, YouTube, or other sources?

How do these behaviors differ across user segments?

What unmet needs consistently appear in user conversations?

Target Users

The primary users for this discovery are Myntra shoppers who:

Add fashion products to their wishlist

Revisit wishlisted products before buying

Compare multiple products

Wait for discounts or price drops

Have uncertainty about size or fit

Look at reviews before making a decision

Search outside Myntra for more information

Delay or abandon purchases after showing initial interest

AI Discovery Objective

Build an AI-powered workflow that can analyze public fashion-shopping conversations and classify them into meaningful product insights.

The system should go beyond simple sentiment analysis and identify:

Wishlist motivation

Purchase intent

Purchase blockers

User uncertainty

Delay reasons

Product comparison behavior

External information-seeking behavior

User segment

Recurring unmet needs

Potential Themes to Investigate

The AI engine should investigate themes such as:

Price sensitivity

Waiting for discounts

Size uncertainty

Fit uncertainty

Product quality concerns

Trust in reviews

Styling confidence

Occasion suitability

Product comparison difficulty

Social validation

Return or exchange concerns

Delivery concerns

Wishlist as bookmarking

Wishlist as genuine buying intent

These should be treated as research hypotheses, not final conclusions.

Success Criteria

The discovery engine should help identify:

The most common reasons users wishlist Myntra products

The biggest barriers preventing wishlist-to-purchase conversion

The difference between high-intent and low-intent wishlist behavior

The most common user uncertainties before purchase

Important differences across user segments

The frequency of major pain points where data allows

The most promising opportunity areas for further product discovery

Expected Outcome

At the end of Part 1, the project should be able to answer:

Which user problems between adding a product to the Myntra wishlist and completing a purchase are most frequent, important, and worth solving?

The output of this discovery engine will provide evidence for deciding which product problem should be prioritized before designing or proposing any solution.
